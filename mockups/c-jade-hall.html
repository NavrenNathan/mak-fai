SEATTLE CHINATOWN–INTERNATIONAL DISTRICT · SINCE 1974
Keeping a living tradition alive, one generation at a time.
Mak Fai Kung Fu Dragon & Lion Dance Association is a Seattle nonprofit founded by Grandmaster Mak Hin Fai. We teach Hung Sing Choy Lay Fut kung fu, perform Southern lion dance across the Pacific Northwest, and pass both on to the young people of our community.
