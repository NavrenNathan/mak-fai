MAK FAI · SEATTLE · EST. 1974

A house with two doors and one drum.

Founded by Grandmaster Mak Hin Fai in 1974, Mak Fai carries two traditions through Seattle's Chinatown-International District: the lion dance that fills the streets, and the kung fu that fills the hall.
