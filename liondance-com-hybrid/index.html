Who we are

Fifty years of lions
in the C-ID

Mak Fai Kung Fu Club was established by Grandmaster Mak Hin Fai in 1974. We specialize in the Hung Sing Choy Lay Fut style of kung fu and Southern style lion dancing.

As Seattle's premier lion dance troupe we perform hundreds of shows year round, and represent the city of Seattle and the state of Washington in the sport of lion dancing at national and international competitions. We aim to enrich and promote lion dancing in the Greater Seattle area, keeping the tradition alive by teaching the younger generations in the community.
