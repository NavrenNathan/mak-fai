Who we are

Mak Fai was established by Grandmaster Mak Hin Fai in 1974. For over fifty years the association has carried two traditions side by side: the Hung Sing Choy Lay Fut style of kung fu, and Southern style lion dancing.

A nonprofit based in Seattle’s Chinatown-International District, we carry these traditions forward by teaching the next generation. Through kung fu and lion dance, our students develop the values that help shape them into the next generation of community leaders.

Support the mission

Why donate

Mak Fai runs as a nonprofit, and gifts from the community keep training accessible, our lions and equipment in good repair, and these traditions moving to the next generation of Seattle students.

Youth & scholarships

Some students and families can't cover the full cost of tuition or gear. Gifts help make sure that's never the reason someone has to stop training.

Equipment & training

Lion heads, drums, training weapons, and uniforms wear out with real use. Donations keep them in good shape and replace what's beyond repair.

Keep it going

Rent, insurance, and the everyday costs of running a nonprofit keep the doors open for classes, lion dance performances, and the community that shows up for both.

Recognition

Awards & honors

Five decades of competing, teaching, and showing up for the neighborhood — and the recognition that followed.

Competition

2026 USA Lion Dance National Championships

Competing in Grand Prairie, Texas, Mak Fai entered teams in both the High Pole and Traditional categories, placing 5th overall in the Traditional Competition with a high score of 9.00.

Honor

Lifetime Achievement Award (2026)

The USA Shaolin Open (USASO) presented founder Grandmaster Mak Hin Fai with a Lifetime Achievement Award celebrating his legacy as a Chinese martial arts pioneer in the United States since 1974.

Milestone

50th Anniversary Milestone (2024)

The organization marked its historic 50th Golden Anniversary with major global martial arts dignitaries in Seattle's Chinatown-International District (CID).

City honor

Mayor Bruce Harrell Commemoration (2024)

Mayor Bruce Harrell proclaimed August 24, 2024 as Mak Fai Kung Fu Dragon & Lion Dance Association 50th Anniversary, honoring Mak Fai's community commitment, cultural leadership, and preservation of Chinese arts in Seattle.
