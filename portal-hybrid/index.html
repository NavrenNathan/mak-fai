Who we are

Mak Fai was established by Grandmaster Mak Hin Fai in 1974. For over fifty years the association has carried two traditions side by side: the Hung Sing Choy Lay Fut style of kung fu, and Southern style lion dancing.

A nonprofit rooted in Seattle's Chinatown-International District, we aim to enrich and promote these arts in the Greater Seattle area and keep the tradition alive by teaching the younger generations in the community — representing the city of Seattle and the state of Washington on national and international stages.
