Where your gift goes

  • Youth scholarships

    No child is turned away. Gifts cover tuition for families who cannot pay it.

  • Gear that wears out

    Hand-made lion heads, drum skins, training weapons, uniforms and sashes — all of it gets replaced.

  • Free community performances

    Lunar New Year, senior centers, schools, street festivals — performed at no charge.

Mak Fai is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and a receipt follows every gift.

$500 and above

Scholarship Members

Give $500 or more in a year and you fund a student’s place in the kwoon outright — and you get a say in how the association runs.

  • 01
    A vote on community decisions

    Members are invited to vote on programming, events, and how giving is spent — the internal calls that shape the year.

  • 02
    The annual members’ meeting

    Where those decisions get made, with the association’s books open in the room.

  • 03
    First word on performances

    Demonstrations, festival appearances and Lunar New Year, before they are announced.

  • 04
    Named — or not

    Listed on the annual thank-you, or kept anonymous. Entirely your call.

A membership year runs from the date of your first gift, and monthly giving counts toward the $500. Membership is a thank-you, not a purchase — your gift stays tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
