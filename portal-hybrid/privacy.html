Privacy
policy

The short version: we only know what you choose to send us, we use it to reply and to keep our records, and we never sell it.

Effective September 28, 2026

Who we are

This website, makfai.org, is run by the Mak Fai Kung Fu Dragon & Lion Dance Association, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit at 606 Maynard Ave S, Ste 107, Seattle, WA 98104. Questions about this policy go to hello@makfai.org.

What we collect

You can browse this site without an account, and we don’t run analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We only receive information you send us yourself:

  • Messages and booking requests. The contact form doesn’t send anything to us directly. It opens your own email app with your message filled in, so we receive exactly what you choose to email, usually your name, email address, and message.
  • Pledges. “Email us your pledge” works the same way. We receive what you write in that email.
  • Donations. Card and digital-wallet gifts are processed by Stripe on Stripe’s own secure pages. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shares details such as your name, email, and the amount with us so we can send a receipt. Zelle gifts go through your bank, and checks come to us by mail.

How we use it

We use what you send us to reply to you, arrange performances and classes, send donation receipts, and keep the records a nonprofit is required to keep, including Sponsorship Membership records. We don’t sell, rent, or trade your information. We share it only with the services that process a payment you make, or when the law requires it.

Other services this site uses

  • Google Fonts. Your browser loads our typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • YouTube. When you play one of our films, it plays in YouTube’s player, and YouTube may set its own cookies.
  • Stripe, Instagram, Facebook, and our school websites. Links to these open their own sites, which follow their own privacy policies.

This site doesn’t set cookies of its own.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything we aren’t required to keep for tax and nonprofit records. Email hello@makfai.org and we’ll take care of it.

Young students

This site isn’t designed to collect information from children. If you’re a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, let us know and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
